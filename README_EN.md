# AI for Teachers

An open Portuguese-language collection of practical resources for educators who want to use artificial intelligence critically, safely, and responsibly.

The initial release includes a beginner's guide, a prompt library, lesson-planning templates, assessment-review tools, and a privacy checklist. The resources are designed for Brazilian elementary and middle school teachers, while the structure can be adapted to other educational contexts.

The project does not collect student data and does not replace pedagogical, legal, institutional, or data-protection guidance.

Contributions and careful translations are welcome. Please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before participating.
